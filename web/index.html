Non-storable forward commitments for compute tokens.

Inference capacity cannot be warehoused in advance. When an engineering team enters a forward contract on Stampede, they are locking in delivery of a committed flow of tokens at a designated future date. Stampede offsets deployment uncertainty by operating a secondary resale marketplace: if a team's production rollout is delayed or volume falls below forecast, surplus tokens can be liquidated back to other network buyers.
